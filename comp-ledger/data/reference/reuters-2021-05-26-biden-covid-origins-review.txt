Biden Orders Intelligence Review of COVID-19 Origins
Steve Holland and Andrea Shalal | Reuters
---
President Joe Biden on Wednesday ordered US intelligence agencies to report within 90 days on whether the COVID-19 virus originated from an animal source or from a Chinese laboratory accident, saying the agencies were split on the question.

Biden said the intelligence community had "coalesced around two likely scenarios" but had not reached a definitive conclusion. He directed agencies to redouble their efforts and report back with their findings.

The order comes amid renewed debate over the so-called "lab leak hypothesis," which suggests the virus may have accidentally escaped from the Wuhan Institute of Virology, a research facility in the Chinese city where the first cases were detected in late 2019.

China has repeatedly denied that the virus originated in one of its laboratories, calling such suggestions politically motivated. The Chinese embassy in Washington said the investigation should be based on science, not intelligence operations.

The World Health Organization sent a team to Wuhan earlier this year to investigate the origins, but the mission was widely criticized as lacking access and independence. The team's report said a lab leak was "extremely unlikely" but acknowledged the investigation was limited.

WHO Director-General Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus subsequently said all hypotheses remained on the table and called for further investigation with better access to Chinese data and sites.

Scientists remain divided on the question. Some virologists say the genetic evidence is most consistent with natural spillover from an animal host, possibly through an intermediate species. Others point to unusual features of the virus and the proximity of the Wuhan lab as circumstantial evidence worth investigating.

The intelligence review marks a significant shift from the early days of the pandemic, when the lab leak theory was largely dismissed in mainstream scientific discourse. Several scientists have said that social media dynamics and political polarization led to premature dismissal of the hypothesis.

Republican lawmakers have been pushing for a more aggressive investigation, with some calling for sanctions against China if it fails to cooperate.
