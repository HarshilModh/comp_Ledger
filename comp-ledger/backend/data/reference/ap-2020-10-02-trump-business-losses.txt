Trump Organization's Financial Losses Draw Scrutiny After Tax Report
Associated Press | AP News
---
The Trump Organization's reported financial losses, revealed in recent media reporting on the president's tax records, are drawing fresh scrutiny from legal experts and political opponents as the election enters its final weeks.

According to published reports, the president's businesses reported hundreds of millions of dollars in losses over multiple years, which were then used to offset tax obligations. The strategy, while legal, raises questions about the health of Trump's business empire and the accuracy of his claims of tremendous financial success.

Legal analysts noted that the gap between Trump's public statements about his wealth and the losses reported on tax documents could potentially create legal exposure, particularly regarding representations made to financial institutions.

"There's nothing illegal about reporting losses on your taxes," said a former IRS enforcement official who spoke on condition of anonymity. "The question becomes whether those same periods were characterized differently to banks or investors."

The Manhattan District Attorney's office has been pursuing a separate investigation into the Trump Organization's finances, seeking tax records and other financial documents through a grand jury subpoena. That case remains active in the courts.

Trump's personal attorney said in a statement that all tax filings were prepared by professional accountants and complied with applicable law. The statement did not address specific claims in the reporting.

The president has broken with decades of precedent by refusing to release his tax returns, initially claiming he could not do so because of an ongoing audit. The IRS has stated publicly that an audit does not prevent disclosure.

Industry experts say large real estate portfolios commonly show paper losses due to depreciation deductions, which allow property owners to deduct the declining value of buildings over time regardless of actual market value changes.
